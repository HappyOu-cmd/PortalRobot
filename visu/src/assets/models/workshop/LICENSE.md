The bundled `worker.glb` is an optimized derivative of the publicly provided
`Animation_Walking_withSkin.glb`. Geometry was simplified with glTF Transform
4.4.0 (`simplify --ratio 0.14 --error 0.002`). The unused walking animation and
unused data were removed. The source's self-illuminated albedo was changed to a
nonmetallic, rough PBR surface; the original 2048 × 2048 clothing/skin texture is
retained. Skeleton proportions and texture UVs are retained. The driver is posed
in the forklift at runtime; no remote asset fetch is required by the application.
